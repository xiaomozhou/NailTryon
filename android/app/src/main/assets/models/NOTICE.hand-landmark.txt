Model topology and original weights originate from Google MediaPipe (Apache-2.0).
Upstream exports recover weights through PINTO0309/PINTO_model_zoo conversions
(Apache-2.0), with no quantization, then export the reconstructed PyTorch networks.
https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe
https://github.com/PINTO0309/PINTO_model_zoo/tree/main/033_Hand_Detection_and_Tracking

The Kotlin inference adapter in HandLandmarkDetector.kt implements the SSD anchors,
weighted NMS and rotated ROI configuration for these networks. It replaces the
upstream Python/OpenCV runtime for this Android application. Original model files
are unmodified; SHA-256 checksums are asserted by HandLandmarkModelTest.

The test assets pointing to the upstream MediaPipe sample photographs are stored
under src/test/resources/hand-pose: woman_hands.jpg, victory.jpg, thumb_up.jpg.
The same Apache-2.0 license applies to those upstream test materials.
